# Templates

## Overview

Consistency is the foundation of successful client implementations and business operations.

This section contains reusable templates designed to streamline communication, improve documentation, and support repeatable processes across onboarding, implementation, and customer success.

---

## Template Categories

### 📋 Project Management

- Project Kickoff Agenda
- Project Plan
- Milestone Tracker
- Risk Log
- Issue Log
- Action Item Tracker

### 👥 Client Communication

- Welcome Email
- Kickoff Meeting Invitation
- Weekly Status Update
- Project Completion Email
- Go-Live Announcement

### 📝 Documentation

- Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
- Meeting Notes
- Knowledge Base Article
- Process Documentation
- Change Request Form

### 🤝 Customer Success

- Client Check-In Template
- Quarterly Business Review (QBR)
- Success Plan
- Customer Feedback Survey
- Renewal Preparation Checklist

---

## Purpose

Templates reduce manual effort, improve consistency, and help teams deliver a professional and predictable client experience.

---

## Goal

Build a reusable library of templates that support client implementation, customer success, technical operations, and business process management.
