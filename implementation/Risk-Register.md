# Risk Register

## Overview

This register is used to identify, assess, monitor, and mitigate project risks throughout the implementation lifecycle.

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Owner | Mitigation | Status |
|----|------|--------|------------|-------|------------|--------|
| R-001 | Stakeholder unavailable | High | Medium | Project Manager | Schedule recurring meetings early | Open |
| R-002 | Scope changes | High | Medium | Client | Document change requests | Open |
| R-003 | Delayed client approvals | Medium | High | Client | Weekly follow-ups | Open |
| R-004 | Integration issues | High | Medium | Technical Team | Complete early testing | Open |
| R-005 | Resource constraints | Medium | Medium | Project Lead | Escalate staffing needs | Open |

---

## Risk Levels

**High** – Immediate attention required

**Medium** – Monitor regularly

**Low** – Track during project reviews
