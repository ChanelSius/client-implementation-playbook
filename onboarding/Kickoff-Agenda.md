## Meeting Objectives

- Introduce project stakeholders
- Confirm business goals and desired outcomes
- Review the project scope
- Discuss the implementation approach
- Confirm roles and responsibilities
- Review the timeline and milestones
- Establish communication expectations
- Identify initial risks and dependencies
- Confirm immediate next steps

## Agenda

### 1. Welcome and Introductions

- Participant names
- Roles
- Project responsibilities

### 2. Business Goals

- Reason for the engagement
- Desired outcomes
- Measures of success

### 3. Scope Review

- Included services and deliverables
- Excluded items
- Assumptions
- Contractual requirements
- SOW and SLA expectations

### 4. Implementation Approach

- Discovery
- Planning
- Configuration or execution
- Testing
- Readiness review
- Go-live
- Post-launch support

### 5. Roles and Responsibilities

- Executive sponsor
- Client project lead
- Implementation lead
- Technical contacts
- Operational contacts
- Decision-makers

### 6. Timeline

- Project start
- Discovery deadline
- Configuration milestones
- Testing period
- Readiness review
- Target go-live date

### 7. Communication Plan

- Meeting cadence
- Status report frequency
- Communication channels
- Escalation process
- Decision approval process

### 8. Risks and Dependencies

- Known risks
- Required approvals
- Resource availability
- Technical dependencies
- Third-party dependencies
