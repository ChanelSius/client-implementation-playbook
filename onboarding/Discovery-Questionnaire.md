
# Client Discovery Questionnaire

This questionnaire gathers the information needed to plan and execute a successful implementation.

## Business Overview

1. What business need or challenge led to this engagement?
2. What outcomes should this implementation achieve?
3. How will success be measured?
4. Are there contractual deadlines or critical business dates?
5. Which departments or business units will be affected?

## Stakeholders

1. Who is the executive sponsor?
2. Who is the primary project contact?
3. Who can approve project decisions?
4. Who should receive status updates?
5. Who should be contacted during an escalation?

## Current Process

1. How is this work handled today?
2. What is working well?
3. What problems or delays exist?
4. Which steps are manual?
5. Which teams participate in the current process?

## Scope and Requirements

1. Which services, workflows, or deliverables are included?
2. What is specifically excluded from scope?
3. Are there required service levels?
4. Are there volume expectations?
5. Are there staffing, scheduling, or coverage requirements?
6. Are there reporting requirements?
7. Are there training requirements?

## Technology

1. Which systems or platforms are involved?
2. Are integrations required?
3. What data must be exchanged?
4. Who will provide technical support?
5. Are there access, security, or authentication requirements?
6. Are there testing environments available?

## Communication

1. What is the preferred communication method?
2. How often should project updates be provided?
3. Who should attend recurring meetings?
4. What is the escalation process?
5. Are there specific reporting formats the client prefers?

## Risks and Dependencies

1. What could delay the implementation?
2. Are third parties involved?
3. Are approvals required from other teams?
4. Are resources currently unavailable?
5. Are there upcoming organizational or system changes?

## Go-Live Expectations

1. What is the preferred go-live date?
2. What must be completed before launch?
3. Who will approve go-live?
4. What support is expected during launch?
5. How long should post-launch support continue?

## Additional Information

Please share any additional context, concerns, expectations, or documentation that may affect the project.
